Accuracy checklist before scanning
  • Use the original camera image at high resolution; avoid screenshots.
  • Remove sleeves and holders, use diffuse light, and turn off flash.
  • Keep all four corners visible against a contrasting plain surface.
  • Upload both front and back; use an angled-light photo to verify surface wear.
